Frequently asked

How far in advance should we get in touch?

Ideally 12–18 months before the wedding. I take on a maximum of 6 weddings a year to give each one full attention. Peak months (May, June, September) book up well in advance.

Do you plan weddings outside Italy?

Yes — we collaborate with trusted vendors in France (Provence, French Riviera), Greece (Paros, Santorini) and Morocco (Marrakech). The Destination package covers these.

How does payment work?

30% deposit on signing, 40% six months out, balance the day before the wedding. Vendors are paid directly by you, with transparent invoices: no hidden commissions on our side.

Do you work with photographers and videographers?

I have a shortlist of 12 trusted creatives I work with regularly, in Italy and abroad. The choice is always yours — I'll present 3 options to match your style and budget.

Can we fully personalise the ceremony?

Absolutely. I work with secular celebrants, priests open to bespoke ceremonies, and multilingual officiants. The ceremony is the heart of the day — we build it together, with great care.
