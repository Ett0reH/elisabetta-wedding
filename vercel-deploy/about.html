Elisabetta.

Born in Florence, raised in the hills of Chianti, in love with old houses and long tables. Designing weddings since 2014.

— On my work

I started by accident, planning my sister's wedding in a stone farmhouse in Chianti. Twelve years later, that turned into 148 weddings, a small team, and a way of working that feels honestly mine.

I think of a wedding as a piece of literature — it has characters, a landscape, a rhythm. My job is to read your story and then find the right way to tell it: with the right people, in the right places, without flourish.

I don't follow trends. I follow you.

— Elisabetta R.
N°005 — Our principles

Five things we believe in.

/ 01

One wedding at a time

A maximum of six weddings a year. In the month of yours, I won't take any others. Your day deserves undivided attention, not a slot in a queue.

/ 02

Transparency on costs

No hidden vendor commissions. You see every invoice, you pay vendors directly, you always know where each euro goes. It's the only way I know how to work.

/ 03

Quiet is a design choice

I don't love installations that shout. I prefer a long table, real candles, seasonal flowers, linen napkins. Beautiful things don't need to be explained.

/ 04

Artisans, not vendors

I work with village florists, tailors, country chefs, and musicians I know in person. Everyone you'll meet on your day has a name and a story.

/ 05

The day after

My job doesn't end at midnight. I come back the next morning to pack the last things, return the keys, and send you home with nothing but the memories.
